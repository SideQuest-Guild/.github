# Getting help

- **Questions about the guild, events or raids:** ask in the [SideQuest Discord](https://discord.gg/nwncP7H9Hh).
- **How loot works:** read [Raiding](https://sidequest-guild.com/raids/) on the site.
- **How to use the site, the bot, the addon or the companion app:** the [handbook](https://docs.sidequest-guild.com) has a guide for each.
- **Something is broken on the site, in the bot, in the addon, in the companion app or in the handbook:** open an issue in the right repo with the steps to see it.
- **A security problem:** don't open an issue. Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).
